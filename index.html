    About this page

    The network. The DWTClassifier of Prof. Chenhui Shao's group (University of Michigan): 528 wavelet features of an ultrasonic metal weld, hidden layers of 150, 100 and 50 ReLU units, and 9 output classes (3 tool states x 3 surface states). 99,959 parameters.

    Trained tab. One of the group's saved checkpoints (stratified split 0), trained on 162 labelled welds. The group's five saved networks score 88.9% ± 2.9% on their held-out test welds.

    Written by an LLM tab. The same architecture, with weights written directly as text by GPT-6 Astra from a description of the process, with no data and no training. This is the method of MIT CSAIL's "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming". Run on 2026-11-06: five replies, 2.2% to 14.4% test accuracy, 8.8% on average; guessing scores 11.1%.

    How it was written. For the LLM-written tab: the exact prompt, Astra's plan, its edits group by group, the explanation it gave when asked two days later, and the rule decoded from the finished weights. Astra's reasoning while writing was not logged, so the explanation is after the fact, not a trace.

    Written from 18 welds tab. Dennis's experiment (branch dennis/usmw-few-welds): not the MLP, but a centroid per condition and a spread per feature on 8 features, written by Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Sonnet 5.5 from 18 labelled welds. About 70% on the 252 held-out welds, against 69% for a formula with no AI.

    Inputs. The real welds are held-out test welds of split 0, standardized with the checkpoint's own scaler. Nine were picked among the welds the trained network reads correctly and one among those it misreads, so they are not a fair sample of its accuracy. The synthetic inputs are not welds: each is the smallest pattern the trained network reads as one class with 99% confidence.

    Private. The weights, the weld features and the prompts (which hold weld values) come from a course dataset that is not public. Please do not pass this page, its link or its data files on outside the team. The published copy is locked: it opens only from the full link.
